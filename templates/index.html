Frequently asked questions

How accurate are the estimates?

Range, time and cost are estimates based on your inputs and station data. Real results vary with speed, weather, load and terrain, so keep a safety margin.

Does it show live charger availability?

Only when a live-status source is connected for that station. Otherwise the plan shows that status is unavailable.

Which connectors are supported?

CCS, CHAdeMO, Type 2 and GB/T. Choose "Any connector" to see every option.

Can I share the plan?

Yes. Generate a PDF, then download it or email it from the results section.
